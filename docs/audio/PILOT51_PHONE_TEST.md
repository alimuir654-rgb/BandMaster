## Owner phone check before inviting friends and family

- Install the final signed APK as an update over V24. Do not uninstall or clear storage.
- Continue the existing band. Check name, lineup, songs, cash, date and bookings.
- Hear Nina's quiet verse and harmony; compare different musicians' ideas.
- Check first playback delay, synchronized parts, master mute, Stop, switching screens,
  screen lock, background/resume and another app taking audio focus.
- Check the formation poster, logo variations and centred Make labels.
- Drag the venue list over its cards, select a venue after scrolling and meet the promoter.
- Write and rehearse a song, play a first gig, then close and Continue again.
- Check a clean installation on a spare device without disturbing the owner's saved band.

Desktop/native evidence covers these core paths and a retained V24 save; it does
not establish physical Android installation, sound quality or phone loading time.

## Friends and family session

Allow roughly20–30minutes. Start an Indie band, hear a few auditions, choose the
lineup/name/look, write and rehearse, then try to reach a first gig. Continue later.
Keep any screenshots or recordings in the private testing channel.

Record phone model, Android version, actual signed build number and whether this
was an update or a clean install. For a problem, record the screen, steps, expected
behaviour, actual behaviour and whether restarting/Continue changes it.

Ask: What was confusing? Where did it feel slow? Which musician felt distinct?
Did the audio fit the person and moment? Where did you want to keep playing?
What would make you stop? Which single change matters most?

Release gate: fix save loss, crashes, blocked navigation and broken sound before
widening the pilot. Artistic acceptance and listening feedback remain separate
from technical checks. No public audio, APK hosting or automatic tester outreach.
