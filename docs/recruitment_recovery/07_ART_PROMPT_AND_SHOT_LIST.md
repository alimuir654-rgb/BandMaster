# Controlled generation briefs — use for real images, never substitute SVG proxies

The following are creative **briefs**, not finished generated assets. A generator may help with original artwork, but output quality/licensing must be checked independently. Use no living-artist style imitation, real musician likeness, misleading photographer credits, fake consent or baked game text.

## Portrait shared style lock

> Character art for a serious British independent-band career simulator. Candid documentary editorial portrait, rehearsal and grassroots venue atmosphere, distinct believable adult British musician with lived-in styling and natural body proportions. Unforced expression, imperfect authenticity, genuine available-light shadows, consistent lens/perspective across candidates, non-glossy skin texture, clean subject silhouette, clear head and shoulders for phone crop. Art-directed as part of a battered backstage contact-book, 1990s–2010s local gig heritage without identifiable trademarks. Portrait itself has no lettering or dynamic interface text. Avoid generic AI stock glamour, caricature, template face, fashion editorial, fantasy/pixel/cartoon look, exaggerated teeth/fingers, over-sharpening.

## Human review control

Generated concepts may have illegible gibberish typography or unbuildable physical details. The art director must correct/decompose sources, not treat the generated one-piece image as shippable. Source trace is essential before publishing any generated art into the game repo.
