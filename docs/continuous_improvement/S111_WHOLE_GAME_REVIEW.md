# Whole game review — 4 November 2026

Game source: `0c92100b826322c8c92fba89317b60b77dc28c9b`. This is a review of the current source, retained native originals and completed journey evidence. V10 is an older phone build and does not contain S99/S100. No game code was changed for this review.

## Overall verdict

BandMaster is a promising playable prototype with a convincing starting-out band identity and carefully protected progress. It is not yet a consistently compelling band-career experience at released-game quality. Its strongest moments let the player inspect and change something specific: Jack’s arrangement, Arjun’s different voicings, an editable lyric, a saved set list. Its weaker moments ask the player to choose an activity, advance a checkpoint and read an outcome without much sense of what these particular musicians actually did.

The principal gap is sustained agency and continuity, rather than the number of menus, assets or passing checks. More genre examples and portraits alone will not close that gap. Finish a representative band and make a recognisable song feel developed across the existing career loop before widening the offering substantially.

This is a qualitative judgement, not a numerical usability score. The three requested personas below are explicitly **SIMULATED**. They are not human playtesters, a musician listening panel or retail-game play sessions. Root reviewed critic/player concerns; a separately scoped assistant reviewed the musician lens. Disagreements were checked against source, including correcting an initial overstatement about absent rehearsal agreements.

That is not a measured claim that these competitors have faster controls or better balance. Their retail builds were not played in this review.

## Ranked priorities

These are player-experience priorities, not newly established crash severities.

1. **Finish a representative band and first career loop.** Zoe plus one contrasting drummer; then recruit, write, rehearse, book, perform and inspect the aftermath with the same people. Protect original prices, snapshots, save rollback and uncertainty. Closure: the task makes sense without knowledge of the development roadmap.
2. **Expose the substantive songwriting path.** The free lyric sketch should be discoverable from the first writing session. Keep other ideas available; do not imply all paths have editable material or label one as statistically superior.
3. **Make existing musical continuity perceptible.** One already-saved song/arrangement should be recognisable through rehearsal, demo and gig, with a few source-supported member responses or decisions. This is a new scoped proposal; literal lyrics and snapshots already persist. No semantic AI, duplicate song catalogue or fake performance is needed.
4. **Validate navigation and practical fit as whole tasks.** UX03, followed by authored profile → recorded circumstances → rehearsal agreement. Repair observed confusion; reuse existing Core/Calendar/owners instead of rebuilding them.
5. **Strengthen the first readable consequence.** Prioritise a complete, specific reaction and meaningful next action; keep detailed metrics available. Review action-dock/body balance in the actual phone states. Normal scrolling alone is not a defect.
6. **Broaden audio and polish after the loop works.** Keep new listening approval independent of written gameplay work. More genres, locations, performers or decorative panels cannot substitute for the preceding work. New art/audio retains its existing approval gates.

The current day plan already puts Zoe and a drummer before genre breadth. This review adds a songwriting-discoverability check and a joined first-career-loop acceptance condition. It does not authorise a broad economy rebuild, another scheduler, a runtime AI system or an APK.
