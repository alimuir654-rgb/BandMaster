## Implemented scope

The Band page opens Life outside the band. A player may explicitly record a musician's current circumstances: unrecorded, a day job with an evening shift every other day anchored on the recording day, family support, or flexible evenings. These are optional career notes keyed by the existing musician ID. Approved cast JSON, portraits and musical biographies remain byte-original. Existing saves start UNKNOWN; employment, wealth, previous wages and contributions are never inferred. Personal finances are not simulated and no family money enters shared band cash.

A request to rehearse on an actual recorded shift day presents a held conflict with the affected musician and public room/time commitments. The player may return, rehearse with available members, or save a plan for the earliest common free evening. A saved plan uses no money or time and does not advance the calendar. Other actual activities can reach its date. The rehearsal can fulfil the agreement only on the exact day with every agreed participant attending; passing the date records a missed agreement rather than silently performing the session.

The attendance adapter preserves the complete canonical roster, indices and life_status. Only actual attendees contribute rehearsal chemistry, trait effects, preparation risk, skill/fatigue changes and performed reaction rows. All-attending/unknown careers delegate to the original Session rehearsal. Repeated exclusions, missed agreements and an approaching booked show can change the consequence of proceeding. There is no universal fixed reward attached to that intent, and no future effect arithmetic is exposed to the player.

## State and transaction boundary

Optional situation_core_v1 holds version/revision, contexts, plans, attendance, agreements and processed action IDs. It is outside demos and completed campaign flags, retains unknown extensions, and survives journal trimming. Unsupported/malformed versions are held without reset.

Context and plan commits save metadata only. Actual rehearsal retains the original £15/one-day transaction, then existing Live/Story/Calendar/Life as applicable, Finance once and one owning save. The UI locks dispatch before resolving; failed saves restore the whole owner/game career and pending input choices. Presentation, resize, reveal, Back and reopening never settle another activity. Original terminal, unfinished-radio and due-show priorities remain authoritative.
