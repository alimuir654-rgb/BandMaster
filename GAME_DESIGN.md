# BandMaster gameplay principles

BandMaster is a band-management simulation with occasional narrative choices, not a linear choice-tree game.

## Player agency
The player builds a band, chooses its members, practises, buys or upgrades gear, manages relationships, makes financial decisions and progresses from rehearsals to bookings and tours. Decisions and incidents interrupt meaningful player-initiated activities rather than replacing them.

## Systemic play
The band has individual musicians with roles, traits, skills and relationships. Preparation matters: a new band should not jump directly from formation to headline gigs. Finances, morale, fatigue, rehearsal readiness and reputation should interact.

## Events
Prefer condition-driven, contextual rules and reusable templates. Ink may supply authored narrative; any future language-model sidecar may add narrative variety but must not directly mutate authoritative game state without validation.

## Visual direction
Clean, tactile music-equipment-inspired interface, reusable Godot UI components and location-specific scene backgrounds; build progressively without requiring commissioned artwork.

## Constraints
Keep costs low, prefer free/offline tools, prioritise robustness and ensure new systems can be tested independently.
