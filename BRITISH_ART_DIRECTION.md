# British launch identity (initial region)

Player-facing title: **Living the Dream**. Internal project remains **BandMaster**. Do not rename save paths or Godot resource identifiers merely for branding.

## World baseline
- Early career: fictional British hometown; small venues and rehearsal lock-ups.
- Currency: GBP; travel by left-hand road network; road scenes feature UK signage, service stations and Transit-style vans.
- Buildings: terraced housing, brick pubs, industrial estates, community centres, local radio and regional press offices.
- Narration: grounded contemporary British English, dry and situational; avoid caricature and relentless misery.
- Progression: local pub gigs and rehearsal work before regional touring and larger venues.
- Genre affects presentation and opportunities independently of region.

## Visual diversity
Avoid using the same furniture, carpet, posters or amber lighting across every scene. Mix bright rehearsal daylight, grey damp afternoons, fluorescent back rooms, neon gigs and late-night travel. Keep UI legible above imagery; favour contrast overlays and avoid baked-in text.

## Social features
The player profile is offline/local in this milestone. Friends, score comparisons, support invites and joining other bands are *not implemented online*. Do not show fake connections, fabricated rankings or working invites. Future online integration requires authentication, privacy controls and server-authoritative data.
